# [2.7.7 / Paper 26.3] Structures don't paste in pregenerated terrain; paste queue stalls

**Environment:** BetterStructures 2.7.7 (MagmaCore 1.29-SNAPSHOT), Paper 26.3 (tested with build 194), Java 25, WorldEdit 7.4.6, WorldGuard 7.0.19, EliteMobs 10.9.8, Chunky 1.5.3.

## Summary
In terrain generated by Chunky, almost no structures appear (0 of 21 predicted grid spots in a fresh ~4,000-chunk area). `/bs pregenerate` places a few in its first ~1,400 chunks and then stops, although "Structures Generated" keeps counting. On Paper 26.3, a chunk loaded with `getChunkAtAsync` is unloaded before the next tick. Two code paths in 2.7.7 expect it to still be loaded then.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Run Paper 26.3 with BetterStructures 2.7.7 and its default content.
2. Run `/chunky radius 488` then `/chunky start` in fresh terrain, with no players nearby.
3. Look at the predicted grid spots, or take a heap histogram: `FitAnything$Preparation` and `Schematic$ClipboardPasteOperation` objects pile up and never paste.

## Root cause
1. **Paste queue livelock (`worldedit/PasteChunkReadiness.ready`)**
   - The queue is serial.
   - When the target chunk isn't loaded, `ready()` calls `getChunkAtAsync`. On a later tick the future is done, but `loaded.isLoaded()` is already false, so `pending` is set to null and the chunk is requested again, forever.
   - The active operation never finishes, so every queued paste stalls behind it.
   - Observed live: a `FitAnything$Finishing` with `pending` completed and `held=null`, while 79 `Preparation` + 38 `ClipboardPasteOperation` objects stayed frozen.
   - Related: only one chunk is held at a time and it is released at every chunk boundary the cursor crosses. That causes constant re-requests.
2. **Scan backlog (`listeners/NewChunkLoadEvent.drainDeferredNewChunks`)**
   - A chunk that has unloaded by the time it is drained is re-added to `deferredNewChunks` and never scanned.
   - 38,708 `LoadingChunkKey` objects accumulated after one Chunky session.
   - 2.1.0 scanned inside the `ChunkLoadEvent` and was not affected.
3. **Misleading pregen stats (`ChunkPregenerator`)**
   - "Structures Generated" counts `BuildPlaceEvent`, which fires before `Schematic.enqueue`. It counts queued builds, not pasted ones.
4. **Watchdog hangs (possibly a Paper bug that BS triggers)**
   - Two watchdog kills, during `ChunkPregenerator.generateChunk` (`chunk.load(true)`) and during player movement next to a BS fit.
   - Both came from a main-thread sync load of a chunk that was also generating asynchronously. The chunk was wedged at `initialize_light` with `generationTask=null`.
   - The wedged chunk leaves a visible hole around the structure.

## Suggested fixes
- `PasteChunkReadiness`: add a plugin ticket *before* waiting, or in the future's `thenAccept`. Better still, pin the whole operation footprint until `close()`.
- `NewChunkLoadEvent`: scan in the event, ticket chunks while deferred, or drop/reload stale keys. Bound the set.
- `ChunkPregenerator`: use `getChunkAtAsync` with bounded concurrency, and count `BuildPasteCompleteEvent`.

## Workaround and evidence
[BSPasteKeeper](BSPasteKeeper/) is a standalone plugin. Through reflection it pins plugin tickets on the active paste operation's footprint (corner + clipboard size for regular schematics/fits, and all module inputs for `ModulePasting$ModularPaste` modular dungeons, up to 512 chunks) and releases them 5 s after the operation finishes. It is used together with the Chunky flag `-Dchunky.tickingLoadDuration=10`.

Same seed and grid, measured with the repo's `diagnostics/tools/bsscan.py`. The workaround test used a fresh 16,129-chunk Chunky square:

| | Without fix | With fix | Control: world generated by BS 2.1.0 on 1.21.x |
|---|---|---|---|
| Underground shallow | 0% | 89% | 87% |
| Underground deep | 0% | 82% | 81% |
| Surface | 0% | 31% | 33% |

With the fix, the paste queue stayed at 0, TPS was about 19.6, and there were no hangs. Full analysis and diagnostic tools are in [README.md](README.md).
